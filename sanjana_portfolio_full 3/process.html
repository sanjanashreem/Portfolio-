Discover

Research

Interview users, review competing experiences and separate assumptions from evidence.

Frame

User understanding

Translate observations into jobs, journeys and task flows that keep the team aligned on real needs.

Structure

Information architecture

Organize content and decision points before visual styling makes weak structure harder to see.

Explore

Wireframing

Test hierarchy and task structure cheaply, while change is still fast and low-risk.

Simulate

Prototyping

Connect the important interactions so people can experience the flow instead of imagining it.

Express

Visual design

Apply typography, spacing, color and component states after the information hierarchy works.

Validate

Usability evaluation

Observe task completion, find friction and prioritize the fixes with the largest user impact.

Ship

Developer handoff

Document responsive rules, tokens, states and interaction intent so the shipped product stays faithful.

Principle

The process is not a waterfall.

Testing can send a concept back to structure. Engineering constraints can change a component. The useful part of a process is knowing what question to reopen — not pretending every project moves in a straight line.
